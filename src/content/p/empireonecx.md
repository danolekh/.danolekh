## What this is

A working prototype I built on my own initiative after reading the company's brief for a redesign. It is a concept, not a paid project, and it is not the company's live website. Calculator rates, case study numbers and open roles in it are samples.

## What's in it

- A dot globe showing where the company's 11 delivery countries are, lit by the real day and night line for the current minute.
- A pricing calculator, a reusable service page template, case study pages, a careers page and a contact page with a slot picker.
- A home page that scored 100 for performance and 100 for accessibility on mobile Lighthouse, with about 30 KB of JavaScript.
