## What this is

A working prototype I built on my own initiative. It is a concept, not a paid project, and it is not the studio's live website.

## What's in it

- A hero that lays the studio's records out along a musical scale in 3D. Each cover sits on the degree its syllable names, and you can drag the track.
- Sections for the work, the room, the services and the people behind them.
